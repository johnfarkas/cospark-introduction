The choice

There are two ways to be in the traditional economy.

You can be a consumer, or an owner.

Almost everything we have been taught prepares us to be good consumers: Pay your bills on time. Keep a little set aside. Put some into a 401(k) and hope the market is kind on the day you decide to retire.

Consumers are set up to survive the system.
Thriving in it takes something else.

Getting Started

Your first step with CoSpark: Participating in the Member Reserve Program.

This is a structured financial growth program with remarkable benefits, funded by corporate revenue. Your reserve stays secure, and the terms are set before you start.

Step 1 of 5 · You

It begins with you.

Every part of the model begins with the member deciding to participate. The reserve and its associated benefits all follow from this.

Step 2 of 5 · Contribute

You make a contribution. CoSpark holds it securely.

Any amount, any time, from $10. The contribution is held in your secure reserve. Every dollar you contribute creates a Point, which is a record of your commitment and activity in the commonwealth.

Step 3 of 5 · Capability

CoSpark members’ combined reserves create real capability.

Because members commit for sixty months, CoSpark can plan against that commitment. Reserves taken together create institutional capability: reserve-backed businesses that generate revenue. And that revenue benefits members in real ways, as you will see in the next step.

Step 4 of 5 · Boost Payment

That capability funds what we call your Boost Payment.

Every time you contribute, you receive cash back from CoSpark-generated funds. Boost Payments come from corporate revenue, paid back to you typically within 24 to 48 hours, with no vesting and no waiting period. A $100 contribution yields a $30.77 Boost Payment. Your $100 stays secure in your reserve. The $30.77 is yours to do with as you please. Contribute more and the Boost Payment grows proportionately.

Step 5 of 5 · Sixty months

Redeem at five years.

After 60 months, in addition to the Boost Payments you have received in response to your contributions, you receive your full reserve back plus a bonus 5% premium.

How these numbers are calculated

Boost Payment equals your contribution divided by 3.25. Effective annual return is the internal rate of return on the full 60-month cash flow expressed as an annual equivalent. It lands between 16.7% and 17.2% depending on the timing convention used for the final redemption, which is why we state it as approximately 17%, and it holds at any contribution amount. Boost Payments and the 5% premium are taxable income and a 1099 is issued annually; the return of your own reserve is not taxable. Savings comparison applies the rate you set, compounded monthly on the same deposits. It opens at 0.62%, the national average savings yield reported by Bankrate on August 28, 2026.

Figures illustrate published program terms and are not a promise of future results.
