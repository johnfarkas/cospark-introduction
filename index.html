The Choice

There are two ways to be in the economy. You can be a consumer, or an owner.

Almost everything we've been taught prepares us to be good consumers. Pay your bills on time. Keep a little set aside. Put some into a 401(k) and hope the market is kind on the day you decide to retire.

Where most of us are

Consumer

Consumers are basically set up to survive the system — but that is a big step away from really thriving.

Where CoSpark takes you

Owner

Owners hold a stake in the structures everyone else pays into. The value they generate keeps building — for them.

The Opportunity

The economy runs on consumers fueling owners.

Real financial strength is created by owning the structures consumers pay into — the grocery store, the apartment, the insurer, the lender. Consumers supply the fuel. Owners reap the reward.

  • Owners — the price of admission
  • A million dollars
  • A charter
  • The right last name

Ownership has always been gated — kept behind capital, credentials, and connections most people never had.

CoSpark is an approachable doorway to ownership status.

The Answer

Defining what it means to be a commonwealth.

CoSpark is a member-driven community, purpose-built to help people move from being consumers to owners. It is an ownership structure built and held by the people who participate — where the value generated stays with the members, and increased participation yields increased ownership.
